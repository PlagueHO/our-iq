## 8. Cross-cutting concepts

## Purpose

Capture concepts that affect multiple building blocks.

## Candidate topics

- Identity and authorization.
- Provenance and source grounding.
- Schema and validation.
- Lifecycle and governance.
- Error handling and resilience.
- Observability and auditability.
- Privacy and data retention.

These topics require requirements and decisions before implementation guidance
is written.

## Open questions

- Which concepts need shared policies or libraries?
- What must be enforced automatically?
