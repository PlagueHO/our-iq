## 6. Runtime view

## Purpose

Describe important runtime scenarios and interactions.

## Candidate scenarios

- Capture or propose knowledge.
- Review and approve a contribution.
- Retrieve trusted knowledge with provenance.

These are discovery scenarios, not committed system behavior.

## Open questions

- Which scenario is the first validation target?
- What are the synchronous and asynchronous boundaries?
- Where are failures surfaced to users or maintainers?
