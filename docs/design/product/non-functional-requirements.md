## Non-functional requirements

## Purpose

Capture measurable quality attributes and operational constraints independently
from implementation technology.

## Requirement format

Each approved requirement should identify a quality attribute, metric, target,
measurement method, scope, and owner.

```text
NFR-0001
Quality attribute: TBD
Target: TBD
Measurement: TBD
Scope: TBD
Status: Proposed
```

## Candidate quality attributes

- Security and privacy.
- Availability and recoverability.
- Performance and scale.
- Accessibility and usability.
- Operability and observability.
- Maintainability and evolvability.
- Cost and sustainability.

These are categories for discovery, not requirements.

## Open questions

- Which quality attributes are release-blocking?
- What data classification and retention constraints apply?
- Which service-level objectives are meaningful for the first workflow?
