## Vision and scope

## Purpose

Define the problem Our IQ is intended to address and the boundary of the next
validated increment.

## Confirmed context

- The initial product context is one team or organization.
- The repository is intended to support a shared, governed knowledge store.
- Functionality and implementation technologies are not defined by the scaffold.

## In scope for discovery

- [ ] Identify representative users and stakeholders.
- [ ] Describe the knowledge lifecycle and governance boundary.
- [ ] Establish the first measurable outcome.

## Out of scope until decided

- MCP tools and resources.
- Storage, indexing, retrieval, and synchronization implementation.
- Authentication and authorization mechanisms.
- Azure resource topology and deployment.

## Open questions

- Who is the primary user for the first validated workflow?
- What problem is costly enough to prioritize first?
- What evidence will show that the problem is being solved?
